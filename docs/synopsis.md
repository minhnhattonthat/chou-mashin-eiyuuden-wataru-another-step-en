# Synopsis

Chou Mashin Eiyuuden Wataru: Another Step (Banpresto, PS1, 1998): a side story to the 1997 TV series. Sources:
ja.wikipedia (超魔神英雄伝ワタル ANOTHER STEP) for the route and twists; the extracted script decides wording.
Episode index = room file (R13.BIN holds episodes 12 and 13). Chapter list for the story pass = these rows.

## Who is who
- **Wataru Homurabe** (炎部ワタル), 10: the Savior of 10,000 years ago, summoned from Ryujin Town (龍神町) to
  Sokaizan (創界山) after a light swallows him near Ryujin Pond. Pilots the mashin **Ryujinmaru** (龍神丸). Treats it
  all like a video game. When the TV-series hero **Wataru Ikusabe** (戦部ワタル) shows up, they are "G-Wataru" (game)
  and "TV Wataru" (rivals, then a double act: 両ワタル).
- **Sakuya Shinobibe** (忍部サクヤ): head of the ninja clan, Himiko's ancestor; refined princess speech.
- **Aragoto Tsurugibe** (剣部アラゴト): swordsman, Shibaraku's ancestor; pompous; once went out with Warujo.
- **Obaba** (妖部オババ): fortune-teller of Monja Village who tells Wataru the legend.
- **Warumonder** (ワルモンダー): the demon who rules Sokaizan by giving every village one single value and forcing
  it on everyone (convenience, idleness, realism, comics, light, electricity, idols, music, medals, fitness,
  marriage...). Each village's obsession is that chapter's running joke.
- **The four demon sisters** (魔界四姉妹), Warumonder's daughters: Warujo (eldest), Warulilith, Warutorin, and the
  youngest, **Warurine** (ワルリーヌ). Warurine joins Wataru as a sweet girl in Ganbozukushi Village; she is
  secretly Warumonder's daughter (do not give it away early). In the finale Warumonder possesses her; Wataru frees her.
